FAQ

Do I need insurance to rent?
Full coverage insurance is required. If you already have car insurance, most insurance companies allow you to use it for rentals.
How old must I be to rent?
18+ as long as the driver has full coverage insurance.
Can multiple guests drive the vehicle?
Multiple guests can drive our vehicles if they have full coverage insurance and are listed on the rental agreement.
Do you offer delivery service?
Yes, we offer delivery and pick-up service for an additional fee. Terms and conditions apply.
Is there a mileage limit on the vehicles?
Each vehicle has an allotted number of free miles that is included in the daily rental fee. This number will vary based on the vehicle model.
Do you offer long term bookings?
Yes, we offer long term bookings depending on the car's availability.
Which cities do you serve?
We primarily serve the New York City and New Jersey area, including but not limited to Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx, Staten Island, Jersey City, Hoboken, Newark, Fort Lee, and surrounding areas. Let us know your location and we’ll do our best to accommodate you!

Why rent with us?

Our fleet is built for the way people move around New York and New Jersey. From city drives to weekend getaways, we’ve got cars that fit the moment.

Booking is simple, service is reliable, and you can choose pickup or delivery. Luxury that feels easy and looks unforgettable.
